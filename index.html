Our pharmacy

YiP: UK online pharmacy & clinic

YiP is Healtha's online pharmacy and prescriber-led clinic. It offers treatment for 25 conditions through a free online assessment, along with a full pharmacy shop. Every order is reviewed by a GPhC-registered prescriber, and medicines are dispensed from YiP's registered pharmacy in Glasgow.

  • Free online assessment, usually reviewed the same day
  • Weight management, men's, women's, sexual and general health
  • Same-day dispatch Mon–Sat, with free delivery in plain packaging
  • Full refund if treatment is not approved
